03The uncomfortable part

Why money gets burnt with zero ROI

✕

Ads before the foundation

Ads send people to a quiet page. They don't trust it and leave. You paid for that visit.

✕

Boosting posts instead of campaigns

The Boost button chases likes and views, not enquiries or bookings.

✕

No tracking

Without the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, nobody knows which ad brought which customer.

✕

Quitting during learning

Meta needs about 50 results a week per ad set to learn who to target. Changing ads every few days resets it.

04How it works

Content builds trust. Ads bring the right people.

Organic

Social media management

Planning, shooting, editing and posting regularly. Your shop window, and what people check before they book a session.

Judged on reach, saves, profile visits. Signals in 4 to 8 weeks.

Paid

Performance marketing

Meta ad campaigns built for one result: masterclass sign-ups, module enrolments or WhatsApp enquiries.

Judged on cost per enquiry and return on ad spend. Stable numbers in 6 to 10 weeks.

The right order: restart posting first, then switch on ads two to three weeks later, once new visitors land on a page that looks alive.
